## Figure handling and interpretation

The site uses 23 figures or original figure panels, with the source figure number, explanatory caption, alternative text, and source link. Figures are rendered from the complete source figure or obtained as original publisher/arXiv images; scientific panels, axes, legends, and data are retained. No scientific content was redrawn. Complete figure panels were cropped out of article pages; explanatory captions are website text. Source credits and applicable licenses appear in `assets/CREDITS.md` and alongside open-license figures.

Review takeaways synthesize the literature and are not new experimental results. Coauthor order is stated where useful; it is not used to infer an individual author’s contribution. Numerical comparisons retain their tested conditions. The PBS discussion distinguishes slow-compression strain limits and inferred network timescales; its figure caption preserves the early high-rate force-equilibrium caveat.
