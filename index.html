02 / RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

Better materials.
Less time to get there.

My goal is to shorten the design and qualification cycle for materials in aerospace and defense. I connect automated impact experiments, physical understanding, and predictive models to learn how materials fail—and use that knowledge to guide what we design and test next.
