02 / RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

Strength, damage,
and failure

I study how metals, composites, and foams respond to impact and shock. My current work combines automated experiments and statistical models to measure dynamic strength, explain failure, and choose which materials and loading conditions to test next.
